I photograph the moments that are easy to miss. The look that lasts a second, a quiet gesture, the way the light hits someone’s face, or something happening in the background that you might not notice at first.

I photograph portraits, weddings, live music, events, and documentary work. The subjects are different, but the idea is pretty simple: show up, pay attention, and be ready when something happens.

I’m not interested in forcing moments or making things feel overly staged. I’d rather let things happen naturally, stay out of the way, and make photographs that feel honest.

If you have something worth photographing, let’s talk.
